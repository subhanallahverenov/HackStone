# SynAIpse Bank Support FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page or contact support.

## How do I transfer money?
Open Transfer, choose source account, enter destination and amount.

## Is my data safe?
SynAIpse Bank uses 256-bit encryption and stores data in secure facilities.

## Supported languages
English, Spanish, French, Azerbaijani.

## Branch hours
Monday-Friday 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM. Online banking is 24/7.
